## Dossier 0.97.2 (pre-release)

This update expands the balance desk and improves duel connection handling. Version 0.90.0 and newer can update automatically.

### Balance and map batches

- Add individual maps to the catalogue and choose their slots from the balance desk.
- Browse catalogue bands from 5 to 10 stars and see secondary slots alongside primary assignments.
- Select a secondary slot while reviewing a map.
- In smaller windows, the catalogue keeps its table rows at their natural height and scrolls together with the lower panel instead of squeezing the table into a small section.
- Owners can manage map batches from a dedicated tab. Importing an `.osz` archive lets you select individual difficulties; failed lookups can be retried through mirrors.
- The balance journal has clearer entries, filters and operator details for investigating stuck duels.
- Restored missing Russian and English translations for the catalogue, journal, batches, archive picker, secondary slots and duel operator controls.

### Kamerton

- Automatic map searches appear in the journal under Kamerton, with the requested slot, star range and number of added maps.
- Training follows the server's job status, reporting completion, unchanged labels, failures and jobs lost after a server restart.
- Model reports show how many test maps would actually be admitted to duels and how many of those predictions are correct, when the server provides these figures.
- Skill explanations use readable names and omit unknown feature levels.

### Duels

- Keep the live duel connection active and make search and acceptance timers steadier.

### Server update

Update and restart the bot alongside the application to use the new catalogue, batch, secondary-slot and Kamerton job features. Older servers may not provide all of these operations or report fields.

Witness remains disabled pending resolution of client-integration restrictions.
