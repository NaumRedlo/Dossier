## Dossier 0.95.2 — pre-release

A reworked pool editor, people who use Dossier shown to each other, a count of who is online, and a batch of fixes. If version 0.90.0 or newer is installed, the app updates itself.

### New

- **People who use Dossier.** Players signed in to Dossier are shown in People even when they share no chat with you. A card carries a "Dossier" mark and a dot when the person is online, and a filter above the list narrows it to everyone, your chat, Dossier users or those online.
- **Who is online.** The quick menu by the avatar says how many people have Dossier open right now.
- **Suggestions from the osu! database.** In the pool editor the "Suggest" source also offers maps from the osu! database near the star rating the slot needs; a pick is downloaded like a pasted link.
- **Categories of your own.** The "Categories" sheet lists every category of a pool: reorder them, pick a colour from ten ready ones, add one or remove it.
- **A beatmap ID is enough.** A bare number typed or pasted in the editor is taken as the ID of one difficulty.

### Changed

- **The pool editor is laid out like the tournament client:** categories stand one under another and their tiles in centred rows of up to three.
- **Buttons instead of the Actions menu:** "Categories", "Backdrop" and "Share" are buttons of their own; a tile with a plus adds a slot.
- **Mappoolers** are a row that is always there: type a name, press Enter, remove it with its cross.
- **Shift and a click** select the whole run of slots between two, **Escape** backs out one step at a time, **Enter** confirms what is asked.
- **Sheets and panels of the editor** now enter and leave with an animation, and a press on a list row is visible.
- **Collections as a map source** show one row per map set with a button for each difficulty.
- **Pool cards** carry neither the publication code nor the issue number nor the length, and show the backdrop the pool really has, from a map or from a file.
- **A pool saved from someone else is read-only,** and opening such a publication saves it first.
- **The pool picture** no longer carries the frame or the balance.
- **The skill names** in Russian are the ones players use, and category colours are stronger.
- **An English first run** no longer starts with a Russian news channel.
- **The "Best plays" source** of the editor is gone.

### Fixed

- **A crash when adding a map** to a full pool that was not the most recent one.
- **Witness reported as missing after an update on Windows:** the app now keeps trying to write its copy out instead of giving up, and stops its own Witness before restarting.
- **Sending a video:** a broken upload is tried once more, and a failure says plainly what happened.
- **Maps that could not be downloaded** are no longer tried again at every start: a map that is on no mirror rests for thirty days, a passing fault for a day.
- **Channel news** are read through the bot's server when Telegram cannot be reached directly.
- **A new account's age** is told in months instead of "0 years".
- **People from outside your chat** no longer drop out of the list for a moment on every refresh, and a failed request for them is repeated.
- **Free slots** can be removed in any pool, and the download line of a slot no longer squeezes its text.
- **Every error notice** is also written to `errors.log` in the Dossier folder.

### Important

- The bot has to be updated and restarted: the online count, the marks in People, suggestions from the osu! database, channel news through the server and readable refusals of a video upload all need the new version.

### Known

- The feed and the ratings still follow your chat only: a person who signed in without a chat sees them empty.
- Titles of people who use Dossier without a chat may not be recounted after sign-in.
- The overlay is unchanged from 0.95.1 and still waits for a check on Windows; the window fix for Alt+Tab is not confirmed either.
