## Dossier 0.96.3 (pre-release)

More responsive replay navigation, lighter controls and smoother idle transitions. Version 0.90.0 and newer can update automatically.

### Changed

- Left and right arrows open the highlighted closed group, then switch between groups. Wheel input responds immediately, and searching centers the matching group or replay without waiting for the carousel spring.
- Group expansion is faster and the background follows the last replay row.
- Ungrouped rows show the map title above the player and date. PP and accuracy remain in the replay details; the mode icon occupies the right edge of the row.
- Search and filters use translucent backgrounds and animate when opened or closed.
- Neutral decorative borders and dividers are quieter, red controls are more saturated, and secondary buttons have clearer hover states. The calculation button uses a shorter label.

### Fixed

- Settings panels and skin tiles remain visible when dragged beyond their scrollable area.
- Workspace and sidebar edges fade gradually. Scrolling shades the full header background.
- The idle scene waits for its first frame before hiding the interface. Waking keeps the scene and its caption through the outgoing fade, then stops its renderer and releases the retained frame. The selected replay preview remains visible during the transition.

### Verification

- GPU (`wgpu`/Metal) previews cover search filters, ungrouped rows, scrolling, sidebar fades and dragging settings panels and skins.
- Regression tests cover carousel navigation, search transitions, drag rendering outside the scroll viewport, idle scene loading and wake cleanup.

### Known limitations

- osu!mania maps converted from osu!standard do not yet have stars, PP or a rendered scene.
- Witness remains disabled pending resolution of client-integration restrictions.
