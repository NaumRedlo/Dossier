## Dossier 0.96.0 — pre-release

osu!mania replays in the app, a darker glass look, Witness split into a core and an overlay, and titles shown for the whole server. If version 0.90.0 or newer is installed, the app updates itself.

### New

- **osu!mania replays.** Replays on osu!mania maps are listed beside osu!standard ones and can be found by typing `mania`. A replay shows its MAX, 300, 200, 100, 50 and misses, stable's accuracy and grade, stars and PP (with "if FC" and "if SS"), and plays live behind its details.
- **osu!mania stars and PP** are worked out on the device by a port of osu!'s own calculators; on 37 maps checked the stars matched osu!'s to within 0.0005.
- **osu!mania videos.** "Create video" and the render farm's workers draw osu!mania replays. The picture is experimental: skins, hitsounds, scroll speed changes and HP are not drawn yet.
- **The mode of a replay** is shown by osu!'s own icon on its row and on a map's card.
- **Titles for the whole server.** The Titles page and a title's sheet switch between holders from your chat and from the whole server; server players show when they earned a title and how long after the first holder.
- **A title's place by rarity** is shown again beside the rarity strip.
- **Witness Overlay** is a program of its own beside Witness Core and has its switch inside the Witness panel; it can only be turned on while Witness runs.

### Changed

- **A darker glass look** across the app: panels, tiles, filters and chips are dark translucent glass, and their grey edges are quieter.
- **Switches in the settings** are rows with a sliding knob instead of a tick in a circle.
- **The replay filters** panel and search line use the same dark glass, and the filter button is round.
- **The PP and hits panels** of a replay take less room; on a narrow window they stand under accuracy and combo.
- **The client** of a replay is coloured: stable blue, lazer red.
- **Video previews** of received videos show the map's cover even when the map is not among your replays.
- **People** are marked "From the chat", "From friends" and "From Dossier"; the "server" mark is gone.
- **A server player's profile** has no "Place in the group" panel; their activity stands under the profile.
- **Long gaps** are told in months and years, and dates under title holders are no longer spaced out.
- **"Videos from others"** uses icons, and the "This device" panel is narrower.
- **Shorter texts** in the settings, the community and the title sheet.
- **Witness is off** until the player accepts the risk osu! support described.
- **Maps are downloaded from mirrors only,** never from osu!'s own servers.

### Fixed

- **A hard line under the header** when a page such as Titles was scrolled: the content now fades into the header.
- **The rarity strip** of a title is one clear track with its place marked.
- **The edge of a switch** is no longer cut off.
- **A video made from a replay sent to the bot** is also sent back to the chat when the account is linked to Dossier.

### Important

- The bot has to be updated and restarted: server players' title dates, the mode setting of commands, osu!mania in its commands, the leaderboard by mode and osu!mania renders all need the new version.
- The render farm gives osu!mania replays only to workers on 0.96.0 or newer.

### Known

- osu!mania replays on maps converted from osu!standard get no stars, PP or picture: conversion is not ported yet.
- Witness records osu!standard only.
- The overlay still waits for a check on Windows.
