## Dossier 0.97.8 – Pre-release №31

This update makes duel picks one click, starts renders for the bot sooner, darkens storyboards behind the play and lets the balance desk enter maps in a sheet. Version 0.90.0 and newer can update automatically.

| Archive | Platform |
|---|---|
| `dossier-0.97.8-macos-arm64.zip` | Mac with Apple Silicon |
| `dossier-0.97.8-macos-x64.zip` | Mac with Intel |
| `dossier-0.97.8-windows-x64.zip` | Windows 10/11 |
| `dossier-0.97.8-linux-x64.zip` | Linux |
| `SHA256SUMS` | Checksums of the archives |

**Duels**
- A map is banned or picked with one click. The 2.5 second hold is gone.
- Progress bars on map cards, the status and room panels and the watched players' fields run along their very bottom edge, like the render button's.
- Duel panels have the app's panel colour and no light corners or rims.
- While you watch a duel, both players' notes, sliders and spinners are drawn, and the cursor feed attaches even when Witness starts later.

**Rendered videos**
- Storyboards are dimmed as much as the background, so notes and sliders stay easy to see over bright storyboards and map videos.
- Long names in the leaderboard run from edge to edge of their row instead of shrinking.
- When a mirror's archive holds an older version of the map, the replay's exact difficulty is fetched on its own.
- Break arrows stay inside the frame on narrow video sizes.

**Rendering for the bot**
- A worker takes a replay as soon as the bot has one, instead of asking every 8 seconds.
- The bot hears at once when a render moves to its next stage, and gets its progress every 5 seconds instead of 15.
- Rivals' avatars load in larger batches.
- catboy.best is no longer asked for maps, previews, map details or pool links.

**Subscription**
- A long plan name runs along its card instead of being cut off.
- A plan whose end date is not known yet says that it renews automatically or that it is cancelled.
- The plan's mark in the account menu has its own colour before the plan list loads.
- Prices are set in the regular face.

**Balance desk**
- A map batch is entered in a sheet of slot and map cells, and columns can be pasted into it.
- Catalog rows fold away when you click a slot's name.
- A catalog cell also lists the maps that hold its slot as their extra one, with their main slot.
- Slots are named the way balancers say them, and HD4 and HR4 are named after gimmick maps.
- Queue and journal filters show only their names.
- Every running duel is listed with a way to watch it.
- The review card shows all of a map's tags.

**Interface**
- The app's letter mark is optically centred in the menu bar and in the app.
- Icons stay sharp at every interface scale, and shrunken pictures are softer.
- Cached thumbnails, feed pictures, moving avatars and video reels are capped, so a long session keeps its memory.
- The left menu's bookmarks meet the window edge without concave corners.
