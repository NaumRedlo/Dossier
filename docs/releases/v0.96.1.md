## Dossier 0.96.1 — pre-release

An intro when the app starts, a reel of your replays while it rests, a quicker and calmer replay list, and fixes for Witness Overlay found on Windows. If version 0.90.0 or newer is installed, the app updates itself.

### New

- **An intro.** Every time the app is started (not when it comes back from the tray) the screen starts dark, the logo appears in the middle with a turning circle under it while the library loads, and the main screen fades in once it is ready.
- **A reel while the app rests.** After two minutes without input the interface fades to the logo, and behind it the app plays replays from your library: it picks one, starts it somewhere in its first half, plays it to the end and moves on to another, without repeating the last twelve. Any input brings the interface back. With replay previews turned off only the logo is shown.
- **Arrow keys light a row.** Up and down move a red frame over groups and replays without opening anything; Enter opens what is lit. Left and right still jump between groups.
- **Drag the replay list** with the mouse; a quick flick carries it further. A click that does not move still opens the row.

### Changed

- **An open group lies on a dark tray** that runs from its title down under its replays.
- **The difficulty tab above a replay is gone.** When replays are grouped by map, each replay row shows the difficulty's stars and name where PP and accuracy were; other groupings keep PP and accuracy.
- **No grey outlines** around groups and replay rows; only the chosen replay and the lit row are framed in red.

### Fixed

- **The replay list froze** when the grouping was changed or a large group was opened in a library of thousands of replays.
- **Witness Overlay is drawn in compatibility full screen,** where testers saw it work; it no longer hides itself there.
- **In OpenGL full screen without compatibility mode** the overlay cannot be seen over the game; the Witness panel now says so and suggests compatibility or windowed mode.
- **What Witness Overlay says** is kept in `witness-overlay.log` beside the app's data, so a tester can send it.

### Known

- osu!mania replays on maps converted from osu!standard get no stars, PP or picture: conversion is not ported yet.
- Witness records osu!standard only.
- Witness Overlay is not seen in OpenGL full screen without compatibility mode.
