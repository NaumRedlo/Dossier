## Dossier 0.97.4 (pre-release)

This update shows where Kamerton may admit maps on its own and explains more duel cancellations. Version 0.90.0 and newer can update automatically.

- The model tab in the balance desk has an Auto admission column. For each duel slot it shows how many of Kamerton's confident answers were checked and how many were right. Kamerton admits maps on its own only where at least 10 were checked and 90% of them were right; elsewhere only reviewed maps are played.
- A cancelled duel now says when the osu! room stopped answering during a map or when the map's result never reached the osu! API. Both end without a rating change.
- Labels in the pool editor, the slot panel and the publish checks use the regular typeface; monospaced type is kept for times, numbers and hashes.

The Auto admission column needs the matching server update and a Kamerton version trained after it; until then it shows a dash.

Witness remains disabled pending resolution of client-integration restrictions.
