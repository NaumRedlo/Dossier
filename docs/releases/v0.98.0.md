## Dossier 0.98.0 – Pre-release №32

This update fixes pictures that went missing, signs the Mac app as a whole and closes holes in links, archives and connections. Version 0.90.0 and newer can update automatically.

| Archive | Platform |
|---|---|
| `dossier-0.98.0-macos-arm64.zip` | Mac with Apple Silicon |
| `dossier-0.98.0-macos-x64.zip` | Mac with Intel |
| `dossier-0.98.0-windows-x64.zip` | Windows 10/11 |
| `dossier-0.98.0-linux-x64.zip` | Linux |
| `SHA256SUMS` | Checksums of the archives |

**Fixes**
- Avatars in Community stay on screen while many pictures load.
- Covers in the replay feed no longer go missing.
- The light line under the head of a score sheet is gone.
- In rendered videos, a long name in the leaderboard ends before the rank number.
- Bright map covers in the balance desk no longer leave a light edge at their rounded corners.

**Mac**
- The whole app is signed, so macOS no longer calls a downloaded copy damaged. On first launch it asks for permission in System Settings, Privacy & Security. Updating from inside the app does not ask.

**Safety**
- Links open only when they are web, mail or osu! links.
- Updates and map archives unpack with limits on size and file count, and an archive with a link to another place on the disk is refused with an explanation.
- Secure connections use the fixed versions of their libraries.
- ffmpeg is downloaded only as a known build, checked by its checksum before it is used.

**Rendering for the bot**
- A device lent to the bot renders only for its owner and for the chats its owner is in. Nobody needs to be trusted by hand any more.
