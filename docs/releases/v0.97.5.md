## Dossier 0.97.5 (pre-release)

This hotfix keeps the balance desk connected and lets reviewers say how well a map fits its slot. Version 0.90.0 and newer can update automatically.

- The balance desk no longer keeps showing "Could not refresh the data" when the server is slow: its reads wait up to 45 seconds instead of 12.
- When reviewing a map, choose how well it fits the slot (exemplary, fits or borderline) and up to three things it tests, such as wide jumps, long streams or low AR. Kamerton learns from both.
- Catalogue cells list exemplary maps first and mark exemplary and borderline maps on their cards.
- The model tab follows a training run: its stage, the sets checked and the training steps. Training keeps running on the server if Dossier is closed.
- Easy and Flashlight slots are gone from the catalogue. Free Mod slots are named after the OWC rules and no longer carry an invented skill.

Grades, reasons and training progress need the matching server update.

Witness remains disabled pending resolution of client-integration restrictions.
