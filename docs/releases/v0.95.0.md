## Dossier 0.95.0 — pre-release

The Pools tab with its editor, publications and collections, a subscription with a bell and a new menu, a feed rebuilt as rows with covers, and the first, experimental Witness overlay on top of the game. If version 0.90.0 or newer is installed, the app updates itself.

### New

- **The Pools tab.** Its main page shows your own, saved and published pools as a showcase. A pool is built in the editor: slots are laid out as tables by category, a map is searched right in its slot, rows can be dragged, and there is undo and redo. Maps come from links, from the game's collections, from best plays and from files dropped on the window; for empty slots the app suggests maps itself.
- **A tour of the editor.** On the first visit to a new pool, step-by-step hints walk through the main things the editor can do. The switch "Hints in the pool editor" turns them off.
- **Publishing pools and collections.** A pool is published under the name of the signed-in player and gets a code; with the code anyone can save it, download the maps they lack and write it into the game as a collection. When a saved pool gets a new issue, the app says so and updates it in place.
- **Pool backdrop.** A pool can take its backdrop from one of its maps; it shows in the editor, on the cards, on the pool picture, and travels with the publication.
- **Pool picture.** A pool can be exported as a PNG card from the share menu.
- **Subscription.** The app has a subscription panel with tiers, a change of tier and the remaining days carried over. The subscriber badge and the tier title show in the profile and next to other players in the Community section.
- **Bell and menu.** Notifications are gathered under a bell next to the avatar, and the quick menu opens as one glass panel. While the notification panel is open, toasts do not pop up; error messages stay longer than ordinary ones.
- **Witness overlay on top of the game (experimental).** The switch "In-game overlay" in the Witness tile. During a play it shows the PP reached so far and the unstable rate above the hit error meter, in breaks it unfolds to the PP of a clean play, and a small tile in the corner tells that Witness is connected. Nothing is loaded into the client: the plates are drawn in Witness's own windows above the game window. Off by default.
- **One Dossier at a time.** Starting Dossier while it is already running (for example, sitting in the tray) no longer opens a second one: the running window comes to the front. Starting another version or another copy closes the running one first and takes its place.

### Changed

- **The feed** is rebuilt as rows with map covers and mod discs, and PP is shown everywhere.
- **Videos** are sent through Dossier; received videos that have expired are restored.
- **The Witness tile** in the settings is shorter: the long description is gone.
- **Witness** keeps undelivered results in a queue between launches and flags rejected ones; the chat card on the song select screen is removed.
- **Interface records** are set in IBM Plex Mono, and star rating pills look the same on pool and profile cards.
- **Presence.** Dossier tells the bot it is running every five seconds, from the tray as well.

### Fixed

- **Windows: the window after Alt+Tab.** The app no longer re-measures the screen or resizes its window while it is in the background, minimised or in the tray, so the screen mode of a full-screen game is not taken for the desktop; it measures again once it is back in front. The smallest window size is also never larger than the screen. This is aimed at the report of the minimise, maximise and close buttons disappearing and the header buttons misbehaving after Alt+Tab; it has not been confirmed on Windows yet.
- Glass panels no longer turn grey on the GPU, and the pool backdrop no longer ends in a hard edge.
- Editor shortcuts work in the Russian keyboard layout; on macOS shortcuts are shown with ⌘ and ⇧.
- Pools and settings are saved more safely.
- The interface scale follows the size of the monitor again.
- A payment that was not created now says why and shows the server's answer.

### Important

- The bot has to be updated and restarted: publication codes, subscriber badges and the check of a pool backdrop only work with the new version.
- A Dossier older than 0.95.0 that is still running in the tray is not noticed by the new one: quit it once by hand.

### Known

- The overlay has not been checked on Windows yet: it is not known whether it is seen in exclusive full screen or whether it costs the game any frames. In a CrossOver bottle on a Mac its windows are created but are not seen above the game.
- The overlay does not show the results of the day, the chat card or your record on the map yet; sending a result to the chat by holding Tab is drawn but not connected.
- The height of the hit error meter, above which the unstable rate stands, is an estimate and may not match the game.
