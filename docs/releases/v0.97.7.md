## Dossier 0.97.7 – Pre-release №30

This update brings a new left menu, a reworked Community feed and livelier rendered videos. Version 0.90.0 and newer can update automatically.

| Archive | Platform |
|---|---|
| `dossier-0.97.7-macos-arm64.zip` | Mac with Apple Silicon |
| `dossier-0.97.7-macos-x64.zip` | Mac with Intel |
| `dossier-0.97.7-windows-x64.zip` | Windows 10/11 |
| `dossier-0.97.7-linux-x64.zip` | Linux |
| `SHA256SUMS` | Checksums of the archives |

**Left menu**
- The menu hides behind the left edge of the window and leaves only small marks. On hover the marks grow into bookmarks over the page, which no longer moves or shrinks.
- Choosing a section, hovering and hiding the menu are all animated.

**Community**
- The feed loads in pages as you scroll and shows three days of a chat's plays.
- Map pictures load for the newest plays first, which fixes feed rows that stayed without a picture.
- Pictures are ready in memory soon after the app starts.
- Your profile's cover lies behind the whole page, and friends' covers lie behind their panels.
- Feed rows fade out at the top edge, as in the replays.
- Scrolling stays smooth however far down the feed you go.
- Best plays show mapper avatars.

**Rendered videos**
- The PP counter climbs the same way as in the overlay.
- The leaderboard moves when a player passes a rival, and every rival is shown on the replay's score scale.
- The difficulty graph no longer has a mark that follows the play.
- A chat leaderboard comes from the chat a player has pinned.

**In-game overlay**
- Ctrl+Shift+O no longer opens the overlay panel. Ctrl+Shift+= and - still change the size, and Ctrl+Shift+H still hides the overlay.

**Replays and settings**
- The replay page no longer has a Delete button. A video can still be deleted from its own page.
- Settings panels go from largest to smallest by default. An order you already set stays.
- The map's background behind the interface is now for subscribers, with a switch in Settings.
- Map previews have their own volume, and overly loud tracks are brought down.

**Balance desk**
- HD4 and HR4 now have standard names: HD Flex and HR Flex.
- Kamerton's reasons use plain names for triplets and beat division changes.
- Who is looking at a card is shown through the shared hover tip.

**Interface**
- A page now fades out as a whole when you leave it, including its panels and the left menu.
- Standing explanations moved into help icons beside their labels.
- Maps are fetched through more mirrors, and a mirror that refuses is rested.
